# **App Name**: Chandra Malti Portfolio

## Core Features:

- Farm Info Display: Display farm information including farm history, location, and contact details.
- Product Showcase: Showcase farm products with detailed descriptions and high-quality images.
- Testimonial Section: Display customer testimonials and reviews to build trust and credibility.
- AI-Powered Content Generation: A 'tool' for generating engaging descriptions of the farm and its products using user-provided keywords.
- Contact Form: Provide a contact form for inquiries and direct communication with the farm.
- Image Gallery: Gallery section showcasing farm activities, events, and the overall ambiance with visual animations.
- Team & Achievement Display: Display information about the dedicated founder and team members, including their achievements, with engaging animations.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Earthy green (#8FBC8F) to reflect the farm's natural environment.
- Background color: Light beige (#F5F5DC) for a clean and organic feel.
- Accent color: Warm brown (#A0522D) to highlight important elements and CTAs.
- Body and headline font: 'Alegreya', a humanist serif font, will provide a sense of elegance and readability.
- Use line-style icons related to farming, produce, and nature.
- Clean and structured layout with clear sections for easy navigation.
- Subtle fade-in animations for elements as the user scrolls.
- Engaging animations to highlight the achievements of the founder and team members.
